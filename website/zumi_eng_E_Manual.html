🌐 Zumi AI Language Setting
(Korean ➔ English)

  • 1️⃣
    Slide the switch under Zumi AI to the right to turn it ON.
  • 2️⃣
    When the eyes appear on the screen, press the 🟢 Green button once.
  • 3️⃣
    When [Recent] appears, press the 🟡 Yellow button 5 times.
  • 4️⃣
    When Settings (⚙️) appears, press the 🟢 Green button once.
  • 5️⃣
    When [Check] appears, press the 🟡 Yellow button 5 times.
  • 6️⃣
    When [Language Selection] appears, press the 🟢 Green button once and change it to "ABC (icon)".
  • 7️⃣
    The language is changed. Press the 🔵 Blue button twice to return to the home screen.
  • 💡
    Tip: The language setting will remain saved even after restarting Zumi AI.
